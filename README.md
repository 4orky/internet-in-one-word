If the whole internet had to be summed up in a single word, this program picks it and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

> **enshittification**

The word earned its place: the American Dialect Society named it 2023 Word of the Year, and it was coined to describe platforms rotting as they squeeze their users and their advertisers. It stands alone as a joke, it speaks about the internet in general instead of one site, it is not the first word that springs to mind, and it is exactly one word. Fifteen hopefuls were drafted by hand and graded against the brief on humour, breadth, freshness and punch; a second read of the brief demoted the early front-runner "feral" — wildness anywhere is not the internet — and "enshittification" took the lead on breadth and freshness, losing less on punch than it gained elsewhere.

## How to run it

    python3 ./main.py
